# Data and map credits

- **OpenStreetMap:** © OpenStreetMap contributors. Database content is available under the Open Database License (ODbL): https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/. Camping and service snapshots retain available source tags. Records are not inspected by KampTrail and may be incomplete or stale.
- **Recreation.gov RIDB:** federal recreation facility data from https://ridb.recreation.gov/. Records associated with camping can also describe other recreation facilities. The current repair does not certify that every record is an overnight campground. Source links are provided when recoverable.
- **GeoNames:** US place search derived from cities5000 under CC BY 4.0. See `data/places-us.LICENSE.txt` for source, extraction details, and checksum. Population is used only to rank city search results.
- **USGS PAD-US 3.0 Public Access:** protected-land access overview from USGS, hosted by ArcGIS. It includes public and private protected lands. Open access does not mean overnight camping is permitted. Source: https://www.usgs.gov/programs/gap-analysis-project/science/pad-us-web-services.
- **Basemaps:** OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenTopoMap, and Esri. These are online services with their own usage terms. The app does not bulk-download basemap tiles or promise offline imagery.
- **Wikimedia Commons:** optional nearby photographs link to the original file page and display available author and license information. Nearby images may depict another location within the search radius, not the selected campsite.

The source code's MIT license does not replace these data, imagery, or third-party software licenses. Browser library license notices are in `vendor/`.
