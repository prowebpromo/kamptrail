About the data

Loading catalogue information…

Sources and dates

Campsite records come from Recreation.gov, the US Forest Service, the Bureau of Land Management, and OpenStreetMap contributors. City search uses GeoNames. Each listing links to its source when available. Forest Service and BLM listings retain the full camping-fee wording and source field when supplied. Use Fee evidence to inspect source records and retrieval dates. A source update date is shown only when the feed provides one.

Loading data freshness…

A refresh date is when KampTrail last downloaded that source. It does not mean a campsite was recently checked. Listings have not been field verified.

Coverage and fees

Coverage varies by state and is incomplete. A missing campsite does not mean camping is prohibited. A public land location does not establish permission to camp.

“Reported free” means the source says camping has no fee. Free water, showers, parking, or day use do not count. “Free with conditions” means camping is free only in some seasons, for some sites, or with a pass or permit. “Fee unknown” means no reliable camping fee was supplied. It does not mean free. Being reservable on Recreation.gov, or a note to check Recreation.gov, is not treated as fee evidence. Confirm fees, access, seasonal closures, and reservations with the operator.

Some Forest Service camping opportunities are secondary activities rather than the main map category. KampTrail includes them only with evidence of on-site camping. Administrative offices, trail access alone and broad multi-area pointers are excluded. Agency points are checked against Census state boundaries; ambiguous or invalid locations are held for review rather than assigned to the nearest town.

Follow current posted limits and closures, use established sites where required, pack out trash, and follow local fire restrictions. Source listings do not replace the land manager’s rules.

Public land, weather, and services

The public land layer and land manager lookup use the BLM Surface Management Agency map. Boundaries can be approximate and do not grant permission to camp. Posted signs and local orders take priority.

Fire perimeters come from NIFC; satellite smoke plume polygons come from NOAA HMS. Layers load only when enabled and zoomed in. Each feature shows its source date and retrieval time. Smoke plumes do not measure ground-level air quality or AQI. Perimeters are incomplete and are not evacuation boundaries or closures. Weather alerts are checked on demand at a coordinate using the National Weather Service. This is not an emergency notification service. Dynamic fire, smoke and alert data require a connection and are hidden offline. Check local authorities, land-manager restrictions and posted signs before travel.

Approximate terrain elevation filters use dated USGS 3DEP interpolated samples at catalogue coordinates. Their retrieval date is not a terrain acquisition date, and grid resolution does not guarantee point accuracy. Missing or coordinate-mismatched samples stay unknown and do not satisfy elevation bounds. Elevation does not establish road grade, clearance or RV suitability. Validated state samples can remain available offline with their source date.

Forecast filters use published NOAA/NWS NDFD temperature samples over the next 24 or 48 hours. Bounds apply to every published sample, at the displayed cadence of up to three hours; they are not continuous or daily temperature extremes. The NDFD file modification time is not each location’s forecast issuance. Missing, incomplete, expired and offline samples are excluded. Weather stays separate from saved terrain evidence.

On-demand forecasts and the separate forecast-grid elevation display come from the National Weather Service and cover US locations only. These are separate from the campsite terrain samples. Topo and satellite basemaps come from the USGS National Map.

Service discovery uses OpenStreetMap tags for dump stations, water fill points, LPG, showers, laundry, groceries and fuel. Expanded category coverage starts with Utah; other areas retain older dump, water and LPG records. Each point shows source dates and access evidence. Opening, RV access, cylinder refills and overnight permission are unverified. No loaded matches may reflect incomplete coverage. Visited, validated service areas can be read offline with source dates; uncached areas stay unknown.

The optional official rest-area layer begins with TxDOT's Texas location list. Travel information centers are excluded from this pilot. Each point links to its source and download date. The separately sourced 24-hour visitor stay limit is duration context, not permission to sleep overnight. Current opening, available spaces and overnight permission remain unknown. Other states have no coverage in this layer yet. Saved rest-area stops retain dated source context; reconnect to check current permission.

RV site lengths, hookups, stay limits, and some fees come from Recreation.gov campsite records. A match means one campsite reports your length and every hookup you chose. Different sites in the same campground are never combined. Road routes, drive times and requested detour estimates use the FOSSGIS OSRM car router. They are not checked for RV height, weight, length or current road conditions. Along-route discovery searches near the road geometry and applies the same reported-data filters as the map. Approximate distance from the line is separate from a driving detour.

Offline maps save USGS Topo tiles for an area you choose. The standard map is not saved, because OpenStreetMap's tile policy does not allow bulk downloads.

Corrections

Use “Suggest a fix” on any campsite, or long-press the map and choose “Suggest a campsite here.” No account is needed. A moderator reviews every report before the map data changes. This device keeps a private link to each report so you can check its status.

USFS road designations

The optional USFS motor vehicle roads layer uses the Forest Service MVUM map service. Zoom in and select a road for vehicle and seasonal fields. District coverage is incomplete and unit dates vary. A designation does not establish camping permission, current conditions, clearance, or RV passability. Consult official district maps, closures and posted signs. Recently viewed road data can remain available offline for the same view, labeled with its retrieval date. Topo map saving remains separate.

Camper reviews and photos

Open “Reviews and photos” on a campsite to read firsthand experiences or contribute your own. Every review and photo needs moderator approval. Visit dates and rig details provide context. Optional dated reports cover approach, noise, shade, crowding, personal comfort and carrier signal. Published community ratings and same-visit observations can filter map and route results. Missing reports stay unknown. Evidence saved on this device can be used offline for up to seven days, labeled with its last check. Observations can become outdated and do not establish current fees, access, safety or camping permission. Carrier observations are not FCC coverage predictions.

Your public name, review, visit date, rig details and approved photos are public. Photo files are resized and metadata is removed. Submissions and photos are stored in KampTrail’s database hosted by Hostinger. Moderators can inspect pending content. Rate limiting uses a salted hash of your connection address. No account or email is required. Keep your private management code to edit or delete a review on another device. Clearing browser data removes the saved code. Reviews and photos require an internet connection.

Routing privacy and offline routes

Calculating a route sends stop coordinates to FOSSGIS through KampTrail. KampTrail does not store coordinates or route geometry in its server database. It keeps a salted connection hash for routing limits. FOSSGIS may log routing requests under its own policy. Successful routes are cached on this device when browser storage allows. Saved routes can be reused offline, with their calculation date displayed. New routes and driving detours require a connection.

Saved stops and offline use

Overnight permission is separate from price, parking and reviews. Open “Overnight permission” for dated, place-specific official evidence and moderated firsthand reports. A personal grant or a stay without asking does not establish permission for other visitors. Initial official evidence covers two BLM locations; other places remain unknown. The report location, visit date, public name and text become public only after approval. Private management codes stay on this device; reports and permission conclusions are not saved offline.

Saved stops, notes, and dates stay in this browser on this device. Export a GPX file to back them up or move them to another device. After an online visit, listings for areas you viewed stay available offline. Map imagery needs internet, except Topo areas you saved with Offline. Campsite name search works offline after one online search. Directions, forecasts, and land lookups need internet. Clearing browser data removes saved stops and saved maps.

Embedded and full-page versions may keep separate saved stops, depending on your browser. No account is required. Standard map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap. Topo and satellite tiles come from the USGS.
