From a place name to a plan

  1. Find an area

    Search a town and state, a campsite name, or coordinates. A specific match opens directly. If several places match, choose the one you mean.

    Search the map

  2. Read the listing and its sources

    Review reported fees, access, amenities and available evidence. Unknown means unknown. Public-land boundaries and a map pin do not establish camping permission or current availability.

    Watch the land-management walkthrough

  3. Save and arrange your stops

    Use Save stop on a listing, then open Saved stops. Put stops in order, calculate a car route when connected, or use external directions. Routes do not account for your RV’s dimensions.

    Watch the trip-planning walkthrough

  4. Prepare before losing service

    Export a GPX backup. While online, download a selected USGS Topo area and use Check saved data. Verify your saved content before relying on it away from a connection.

    Watch the offline walkthrough

What to expect from the beta

Saved stops live in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data or storage eviction can remove them. Home Screen installation may help storage retention, but a GPX backup is still important.

New routes, fresh weather, uncached listings and sending feedback need a connection. KampTrail is a planning aid. Confirm current access, closures, fees and vehicle restrictions with the operator or land manager.
