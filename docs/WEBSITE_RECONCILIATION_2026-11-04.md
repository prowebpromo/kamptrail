# KampTrail website reconciliation

Owner request: evaluate the Roam Meet Explore website and build what KampTrail needs, using existing Boondock or Bust resources where appropriate. This is separate from the twenty-item Phase 1 remediation SOW. The comparison below describes published website claims, not an independently tested RME app.

Subsequent owner direction: BoB acquires editorial search traffic; KampTrail validates utility use and retention. The matrix is retained as a record of the earlier comparison, not a mandate for new editorial or SEO pages. `BETA_INTEGRATION_2026-11-04.md` governs the current beta priorities.

## Section-by-section decision

| RME section | Observed website purpose | Existing KampTrail / BoB coverage | Decision and implementation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Roam | Explains place discovery, filters, saved places and optional trip recording | Working campsite map, filters, saved stops, routes, layers and GPX; no background travel recording | Add `plan.html`, an original, shareable explanation of the actual KampTrail workflow and its limits. Do not advertise background recording. |
| Meet | Promotes traveler profiles, location sharing, messages and meetups | Campsite reviews/reports, without traveler accounts or messaging | Do not add a Meet tab. Profiles, safety operations, consent and moderation would be a separate product project. |
| Explore | Promotes posts, short videos, builds and creator guides | How-to videos, BoB editorial guides and resource directory | Improve the existing Guides path and link the new planning page to it. Do not imply a traveler feed exists. |
| Market | Promotes member vehicle, gear and service listings and conversations | BoB product information; no classifieds workflow | Do not publish a marketplace or an empty Market page. Seller identity, reporting and ongoing moderation are not in the current scope. |
| Creators | Recruits creator feedback and promotes creator pages/tools | Founder identity, private beta feedback and BoB content | Add `about.html` with the existing approved founder account, photo and a concrete beta-testing invitation. No payout, audience or promotion promises. |
| Blog | Publishes editorial guides | BoB already publishes RV/camping articles | Keep BoB as the editorial destination through `guides.html`. Do not copy or duplicate its articles onto KampTrail. |
| News | Publishes stories and an external-headline feed | KampTrail has `updates.html` for release notes; BoB has editorial coverage | Retain release notes and clear navigation. A regularly maintained travel-news operation is not necessary to explain this beta. |
| Events | Lists dated gatherings with locations and organizer links | No maintained KampTrail events database or attendance tools | Defer a dedicated calendar. Dates and cancellations require an owner and maintenance process. Do not seed it by copying another publisher's listings. |
| Free tools | Groups no-account planning tools by task | BoB trip-cost, power, water, maintenance and worksheet resources already linked from KampTrail | Organize `tools.html` into trip preparation, power/water and RV upkeep, with clear formats and external destinations. Do not duplicate working BoB calculators or imply every BoB service is account-free. |

## Website deliverable

The public website now has distinct planning and founder pages, task navigation, consistent links between the map, planning help, Tools and Guides, original product explanations, complete social metadata and page/collection structured data. The existing photographic homepage is preserved. New pages are packaged for offline navigation and listed in the sitemap.

This does not represent feature parity with RME. The objective is a useful KampTrail website that explains the existing product and takes visitors to a working next step. No empty navigation destinations, copied RME assets, fabricated events or new social accounts are introduced.
