# KampTrail MVP usability session

Purpose: determine whether RVers can find a plausible campsite, understand its limits, and save a stop without assistance. Automated regressions cover behavior; they do not establish human usability. No real-RVer sessions have been conducted for this release.

Recruit five RVers who have not used KampTrail. Use their own phones and browsers. Run each session for 15–20 minutes. Ask them to think aloud. Do not explain controls or suggest a path before an attempt. Record device/browser, task completion without help, time, missteps, and the participant’s exact feedback. Do not collect precise home locations.

1. “You are planning a stop near Albany, New York. Find a campsite you would investigate.” Observe how they start and whether they find the map/list.
2. “Find options reported as free. What would you still need to check?” Pass when the participant distinguishes reported free from fee unknown and checks permission/current conditions with the source.
3. “Open a campsite and find its coordinates and original information.” Record whether source links and limitations are understandable.
4. “Keep this campsite so you can return to it, then reload the page and find it again.” Observe Saved stops discovery and whether local/device-only storage is understood.
5. “Get directions to this campsite.” Stop before actual travel. Confirm the correct destination is passed to the directions provider.
6. “Would this work if your phone lost service? Show where you would find out.” Record whether they distinguish cached listings from unavailable map imagery/directions.

Afterward ask: What was confusing? What information was missing before you could decide to visit? Would you use this again, and why?

Prioritize any issue that prevents task completion, causes an incorrect free/permission/offline assumption, or hides source information. A practical release target is at least four of five participants completing tasks 1, 3, 4 and 5 without assistance, with no participant interpreting unknown fees as free or the map as proof of camping permission. Fix observed failures before adding optional features.
