## Why the row does not become complete

The adopted scope in [RIDB_AMENITY_EVIDENCE.md](../RIDB_AMENITY_EVIDENCE.md) explicitly retains broader agency amenities as partial. The expansion adds six RIDB campsite-record groups: fire fixtures, grills, trash, food lockers, tent pads and the separate mixed field. It does not add a complete facility inventory or reviewed structured USFS/BLM amenity arrays. Missing/current availability stays unknown, and record mentions do not become campground-wide claims or public RV services.

The inherited matrix label, “Place detail: amenities list,” has no frozen list of mandatory amenity types or numeric coverage target. That missing criterion limits any claim that one more data release completes it. If the intended user job is narrowed to displaying available RIDB record mentions with honest qualifiers, the current implementation supports that narrower job. Narrowing the criterion would be a scope decision rather than new implementation credit earned by deploying 2.20. Preserve the existing partial status until the broader requirement is explicitly resolved.
