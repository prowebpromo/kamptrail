## Method and limits

The browser check was read-only. It sent no POST, PUT or DELETE requests to kamptrail.com and submitted no reviews, ratings or videos. No page errors were recorded. Moderated publication, video playback and seasonal averaging were not exercised against production data.

The 2.11.1 fee evidence remains intact in 2.19.0: 33,286 sites (2,153 free, 59 conditional, 8,290 paid, 22,784 unknown), all ten reviewed USFS records free, and Tower Rock Recreation Site paid.

## Gap outside this score

Fee completeness is not a parity row, because FreeRoam did not publish it either. It is still KampTrail's largest data gap: 22,784 of 33,286 sites (68%) have unknown fees. The ten 2026-10-30 USFS fee reviews expire for imports on or after 2026-11-29. The weekly import runs Sundays at 03:00 UTC, and 2026-11-29 is a Sunday.
