# General land care guidance

Criterion: “Leave No Trace / respect the land guidance.”

The integrated About the data dialog retains one Camping care checklist (`#kt-camping-care`), also reachable from map spot help: concise general guidance about campsite surfaces, litter and waste, fire, wildlife, other visitors and leaving natural/cultural objects in place. Links point to the primary sources below. The guidance is part of index.html and remains readable with the saved app shell; external sources require a connection.

Current land-manager rules, local orders and posted signs control camping permission, stay duration, fire use and waste requirements. No general legal distance, stay limit or fire permission was added. Existing agency-specific spot-check guidance is unchanged. The review date concerns the general text, not current restrictions, opening or permission at a campsite. This package does not monitor closures or establish site-level compliance.

The guide is original concise wording, not a reproduction of the seven principles or their full explanatory text. It does not claim affiliation or endorsement.
