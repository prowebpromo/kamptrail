## Decisions needed from the owner

1. Withdraw the nine. The reviewer recommends withdrawal for all nine because neither the retained nor the refreshed own record establishes camping. Their labels came from matched USFS or OSM records, which are candidate evidence only. Withdrawing Daisy State Park and River Forks would also withhold their absorbed OSM elements (osm:way:941066426, osm:relation:13942065).
