This is a modified extract containing only records whose country code is US.
It retains GeoNames ID, original primary place name, USPS state abbreviation,
WGS84 latitude/longitude and source population. Alternate names are limited
to ASCII strings for a compact English-language search index. Exact primary
name duplicates are removed from aliases. Records are sorted by descending
population, then state/name/ID. No coordinates or population values were
invented. The source population is for search ranking, not a current census
claim. No population field is displayed in the interface.

The cities5000 source generally includes cities with population over 5,000
and first-order administrative seats. It is not a complete list of every
US community, neighborhood, address, campground or postal code. An absent
place does not mean it does not exist. This extract contains 7,557 places
across all 50 US states and the District of Columbia. Territories are not
included because their GeoNames country codes differ from US.
